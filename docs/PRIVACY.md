# LogLift Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

[简体中文](privacy/zh-Hans.md) · [日本語](privacy/ja.md) · [한국어](privacy/ko.md)

LogLift is published by huihuisang. This policy covers the native macOS app, LogLift for DJI, and its Finder extension.

## Local video processing

LogLift reads the videos you select and converts them on your Mac. It does not upload your videos, audio, filenames, or media metadata to the developer. No account is required. The app does not send analytics or session recordings to the developer and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Settings and file access

The app stores preferences and file access bookmarks on your Mac. These include camera order, export settings, the output folder, and folders you approve for Finder imports. The app uses the file access you grant through macOS. It does not request access to your whole disk.

You can disable notifications and the Finder extension in System Settings. You can remove saved preferences and file access bookmarks from the app's local container after you close the app. If you remove these records, the app must request access again when needed.

## File retention

Your source videos stay in their original locations. Completed exports stay in the folder you select until you delete them. Removing a clip from the queue does not delete its original or completed export. A failed or cancelled conversion removes its temporary output when the app can complete cleanup. If the app is force quit or the system stops it, a partial output can remain in the export folder.

The developer does not hold copies of your selected videos or local preferences. Files outside the app's container, including completed exports, must be deleted separately if you no longer want them.

## External links and support

The app's website link and privacy link open your browser. The destination website handles its own requests under its privacy policy. This policy is hosted on GitHub; [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies to use of that website. The developer website has its own privacy practices.

For support or a privacy question, open an issue at [the LogLift project](https://github.com/huihuisang/DJI-LogLift/issues). GitHub handles information you send through its service. Issues can be public, so do not post private videos, personal data, or credentials. The app does not send a support request or attach a video on your behalf.

## Policy changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated. App Store privacy answers will be updated when required.
